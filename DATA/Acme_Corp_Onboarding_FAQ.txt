ACME CORPORATION - NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING FAQ
Effective: January 2026

Q1: When will I receive my laptop and equipment?
A: IT will issue your laptop, headset, and access badge on your first day. If you are joining remotely, equipment is couriered to your registered address at least 3 days before your joining date.

Q2: How do I set up my company email and Slack account?
A: Your company email (firstname.lastname@acmecorp.example) is created automatically 24 hours before your joining date. Login credentials are sent to your personal email. Slack workspace invites follow within the first 2 hours of your first day.

Q3: What is covered in the first week of onboarding?
A: Week 1 includes: HR orientation session, IT systems setup, introduction to your reporting manager and team, mandatory compliance training (Code of Conduct, POSH, Data Security), and a benefits enrollment walkthrough.

Q4: When do my benefits (insurance, etc.) start?
A: Health insurance coverage begins from your date of joining. You must complete benefits enrollment through the HR portal within the first 15 days, or default coverage will be applied automatically.

Q5: Who do I contact if I face any onboarding issues?
A: Reach out to your assigned HR buddy (introduced on Day 1) or email onboarding@acmecorp.example. For urgent IT issues, contact the IT helpdesk at extension 4040.

Q6: Is there a probation period?
A: Yes, all new employees undergo a 6-month probation period as outlined in the Employee Leave and Time-Off Policy. Your manager will conduct a formal review before confirmation.

Q7: How do I apply for leave during probation?
A: You may apply for casual and sick leave on a pro-rata basis during probation via the HR portal. Earned leave encashment is not available until after confirmation.

Q8: What is the reporting structure I should know on Day 1?
A: You will report directly to the hiring manager communicated in your offer letter. Org charts are available on the internal HR portal under "My Team."

Q9: When is the first salary credited?
A: Salaries are processed on the last working day of each month. If you join mid-month, your first salary will be pro-rated based on your joining date.

Q10: Where can I find the complete list of company policies?
A: All HR policies, including Leave Policy, Code of Conduct, and Expense Policy, are available on the HR portal under "Policies & Documents."
